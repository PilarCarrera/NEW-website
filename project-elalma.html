Women's Theatre Collective Website

El Alma de la Manzana

I designed and developed the website for El Alma de la Manzana, a social, cultural and theatre project formed by a collective of women, born in 2020 in Villanueva de Villaescusa (Cantabria). The site gives the group a home online to present who they are, their play, their upcoming and past performances, their gallery and their press coverage.

UI/UX Design Front-End Development Cultural Project

Project Snapshot

Role: UI/UX Designer and Front-End Developer

Team: Individual contributor

Location: Cantabria, Spain

Context

The group is a local, Spanish-speaking theatre collective, so all website content is presented in Spanish. The visual identity is built around the hand-drawn apple from their poster, with soft lilac, cream and pink tones that match the spirit of the play.

My Role

I owned the project end to end: information architecture, visual direction, responsive UI design and front-end implementation, from concept through final delivery.

Process

I structured a one-page scrolling experience with sections for who they are, the play, upcoming performances, gallery, past performances, press and contact, plus dedicated gallery and press pages. Interactive pieces include an apple-shaped jigsaw puzzle whose pieces reveal the women featured in the play, an apple-themed calendar for upcoming shows, and a clickable map of Cantabria that opens the venue and date of each past performance.

Outcome

Delivered a complete Spanish-language website, fully implemented for both desktop and mobile. The project includes additional sections beyond the screenshots shown here, but only a subset is displayed in this portfolio.

Tech Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive UI implementation.
