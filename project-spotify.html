UI/UX Redesign & Development

Spotify Magical Redesign

I redesigned the Spotify UI/UX into something cutesie and magical, complete with fairy dust. The redesign focuses on a delightful visual identity while keeping core functionality intact: you can add and delete songs, create playlists, and search for them. And yes, it actually works, I programmed it too.

UI/UX Redesign Front-End Development Magical Vibes

Context

I wanted to explore what Spotify would look like if it leaned into a playful, magical aesthetic, soft gradients, sparkle effects, fairy dust. The opposite of Spotify's current minimal dark mode, done entirely for fun and creative exploration.

My Role

I designed and built everything. I designed the full UI/UX in Figma first, then programmed it as a working web app. The app lets you add and delete songs, create playlists, and search through them, it's not just a mockup.

Outcome

A fully functional music player UI/UX with a dreamy, cutesie visual identity. One of my favourite personal projects, proof that utility and whimsy aren't mutually exclusive.
