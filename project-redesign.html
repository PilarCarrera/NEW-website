UI/UX Redesign Product Website Visual System

Context

iSYS Software GmbH had an outdated product website that no longer reflected the quality of their software. They wanted a modern, credible web presence to better communicate their offering to potential clients.

My Role

I led the visual redesign end to end, defining a new component system, typography hierarchy, and layout structure, then producing the designs in Figma. I created both a brand new landing page and reworked the internal page layouts.

Outcome

A clean, consistent design system with a redesigned landing page and internal pages that communicate the company's technical expertise far more clearly than the original site. The main improvement was UX clarity: labels and section names were renamed to be more intuitive, actions were placed where users expected them, and the click flow became easier to follow so users were not as lost as before.
